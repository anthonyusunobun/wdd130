Our purpose is to provide thrilling and safe rafting experiences that connect people with nature while respecting the river.

History

Founded in 1998 with a single raft and a passion for adventure, our company began on the Colorado River.

Over 25 years, we have guided over 50,000 guests safely down the rapids and built a legacy of adventure.
